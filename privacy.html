Inkwell – Privacy

Inkwell is a private notes app run by one person for a small group of friends. It is not a commercial service.

What is stored

Your notes, notebooks and settings are stored on your device and synced to a Supabase database so they appear on your other devices. Pictures are stored either in Inkwell's shared cloud storage (Cloudflare R2) or, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. Your email address is used only to sign you in.

Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, Inkwell asks only for the drive.file permission. That lets it see and change only the files Inkwell itself created (your Inkwell pictures folder), nothing else in your Drive. It also reads your Google email address to show which account is connected. A sign-in token is kept on Inkwell's server so pictures can sync; it is used for nothing else. You can disconnect in Inkwell's settings at any time, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Inkwell's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What is not done

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and no data is sold or shared with anyone. Notes are only sent to an AI service when you use the Ask feature yourself.

Deleting your data

Ask the person who invited you to delete your account; all notes, pictures in Inkwell's cloud and the saved Google sign-in are removed with it. Pictures in your own Google Drive stay yours.
